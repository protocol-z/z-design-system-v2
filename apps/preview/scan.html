Scanner v2 · Tier 1 components

The calmest block explorer on the internet.

14 new primitives + a Tabs extension, all on v2 tokens. This page is the visual reference design and engineering should sign against before the Z Scan restyle starts.

01 · TypeBadge

Six tones, two sizes, one rule.

Dense tables get the small badge — dot on a paper pill. Detail-page headers get the medium badge — full color fill. Same data, different voice depending on the surface.

Small (table-row size)

.tb--sm

Dot + label on neutral pill. Used in the latest-transactions feed and any tx list. Reads as a quiet category marker, not a status alert.

Medium (detail-page header)

.tb--md

Color-fill background. One per page near the Status field. Reads as the primary classification of the transaction.

02 · AddressLabel · HashChip

Named addresses read first, hex stays close.

The MASP Pool address shouldn't look like a random contract. AddressLabel gives any known address a tone, a label, and the hex tail for verification.

03 · InlineNotice

Empty by design, never apologetic.

The "Amounts hidden" notice gets its own component because privacy explanations carry product weight. Mint wash, calm copy, never a warning banner.

Transfer details are private. Amounts and recipients are encrypted within the MASP pool.
Shielded transfers are not listed here. No token transfer event is emitted for within-pool transfers.
Stale quote. Refresh to get the current swap path before you confirm.
Transaction reverted. The on-chain call ran out of gas at step 3. No funds left your wallet.

04 · SectionLabel

Translate cryptography into trust.

The header strip that gives a MASP log entry plain-English context. Caption typography, optional accent dot, a soft mist track. Sits above DataTable rows or KeyValueGrid blocks.

MASP commitment insertion A new note was added to the shielded pool.
MASP note transfer A note was spent and a new note created — neither side is observable.
MASP nullifier spend A note exited to a transparent address.

10 · Chart kit

Privacy metrics, finally legible.

Eight chart primitives on the v2 palette. Capped at four series so the page never reads as a color zoo. Tonal numerals, dotted-grid grounds, paper plates with shadow.

LineChart — Daily Shield Volume

.zchart-line

Single-series line with mint area fill on a dotted-grid plate. The default treatment for any time-series where the *number* is the story.

AreaChart — Daily MASP Transactions, by type

.zchart-area

Stacked bottom-up: Shield (mint) + Transfer (moss) + Unshield (stone). One legend pill per series, capped at four total.

BarChart — Hourly transactions

.zchart-bar

Single-series vertical bars, slim radius, ink fill. Use for hour/day discrete buckets.

BarChart — x402 vs standard, daily

.zchart-bar-grouped

Grouped bars, two series. Yellow for x402 (campaign accent), stone for standard rails.

DonutChart — Privacy Ratio

.zchart-donut

Two-segment composition with a center metric. The flagship "privacy index" visual for the home page and deck.

Sparkline — inline trend

.zchart-spark

Tiny line for stat tiles and table cells. Tones inherit positive / negative / neutral / private.
